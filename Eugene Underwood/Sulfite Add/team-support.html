Sulfite Add support

Sulfite Add is a free iOS app from Eugene Underwood. Enter the barrel, the pH, and the free SO2 already in the wine. The app shows the free SO2 target and the potassium metabisulfite to add. A pH above the hold line is marked Hold and does not show a weight.

Barrels and the addition log stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

Contact Eugene Underwood at eugene.underwood79@sina.com. The contact address is United States.
