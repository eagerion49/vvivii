Privacy Policy for Sulfite Add

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Sulfite Add is a free iOS app from Eugene Underwood. It turns a barrel volume, pH, and current free SO2 into a potassium metabisulfite addition. Barrels and logged additions stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. Contact Eugene Underwood at eugene.underwood79@sina.com. The contact address is United States.
