Privacy Policy

Pitot Traverse

Effective date: 2026-11-22

Developer: Arthur Lawson (arthur.lawson42@sina.com)

Pitot Traverse is an offline HVAC duct traverse tool for iPhone. It does not require an account, does not include in-app purchases, and does not track you.

Traverse grids, velocity readings, design airflow, and exported text or CSV reports stay on this iPhone unless you choose to share a file through the system share sheet.

Location is optional. If you allow location while using the app, the jobsite coordinates are saved with that traverse on this iPhone only. The app does not load an online map and does not upload the coordinates.
