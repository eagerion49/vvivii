Privacy Policy for Jar Dose

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Jar Dose is a free iOS app from Ralph Crawford. It picks a coagulant dose from jar-test readings on this iPhone. Jar readings, chemicals, and saved trials stay on the device. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. Contact Ralph Crawford at ralph.crawford34@gmail.com. The contact address is United States.
