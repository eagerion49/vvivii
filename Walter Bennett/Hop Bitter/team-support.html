Hop Bitter support

Hop Bitter is a free iOS app from Walter Bennett. Enter the hops, the kettle gravity, and each addition. The app shows bitterness for the boil and whether it sits inside the style band. A zero-minute addition is aroma only.

Hops and saved boils stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

Contact Walter Bennett at walter.bennett40@sina.com. The contact address is United States.
