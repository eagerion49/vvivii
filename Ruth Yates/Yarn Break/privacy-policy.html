Privacy Policy for Yarn Break

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Yarn Break is a free iOS app from Ruth Yates. It keeps the yarn breaks that count and averages their tenacity. Lots stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. Contact Ruth Yates at ruth.yates37@sina.com. The contact address is United States.
