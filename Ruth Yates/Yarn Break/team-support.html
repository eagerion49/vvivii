Yarn Break support

Yarn Break is a free iOS app from Ruth Yates. Enter each strand’s force, extension, and jaw distance. A break within 3 mm of a jaw stays out of the mean only when its force is under 20% of the other breaks. Tenacity is force divided by tex.

Lots stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.
