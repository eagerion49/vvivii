Privacy Policy for Yeast Pitch

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Yeast Pitch is a free iOS app from Walter Keller. It turns wort gravity, volume, and the age of a yeast pack into a starter size. Packs and batches stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. Contact Walter Keller at walter.keller40@outlook.com. The contact address is United States.
