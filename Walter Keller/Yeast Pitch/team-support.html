Yeast Pitch support

Yeast Pitch is a free iOS app from Walter Keller. Enter the wort and the yeast pack. The app shows how many live cells you have and the smallest starter that reaches the pitch. A pack older than the brew day is blocked.

Packs and batches stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

Contact Walter Keller at walter.keller40@outlook.com. The contact address is United States.
