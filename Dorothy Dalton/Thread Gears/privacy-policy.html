Privacy Policy for Thread Gears

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Thread Gears is a free iOS app from Dorothy Dalton. It finds a lathe change-gear train on this iPhone. Gear sets, leadscrews, and saved trains stay on the device. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. Contact Dorothy Dalton at dorothy.dalton55@outlook.com. The contact address is United States.
