Privacy Policy for Turn Pace

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Turn Pace is a free iOS app from Eugene Crawford. It keeps each blank’s diameter, surface speed, feed, and the cut minutes on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. A table you choose, and a photo you take of a blank, stay on this iPhone. Contact Eugene Crawford at eugene.crawford81@163.com. The contact address is United States.
