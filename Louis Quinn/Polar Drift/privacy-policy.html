Privacy Policy for Polar Drift

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Polar Drift is a free iOS app from Louis Quinn. It turns a star's drift into a polar-axis correction on this iPhone. Sites and readings stay on the device. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. Contact Louis Quinn at louis.quinn75@163.com. The contact address is United States.
