Flat Field support

Flat Field is a free iOS app from Margaret Zimmerman. Enter a test exposure. The app subtracts the bias, scales the time to the target, and picks the lowest panel level that lands between 1 and 10 seconds.

A test that is already near full scale is marked saturated and is not scaled. Filters and nights stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.
