Privacy Policy for Flat Field

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Flat Field is a free iOS app from Margaret Zimmerman. It turns a test exposure into a flat-frame time, then picks a panel level for each filter. Cameras, filters, and saved nights stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. Contact Margaret Zimmerman at margaret.zimmerman36@yahoo.com. The contact address is United States.
