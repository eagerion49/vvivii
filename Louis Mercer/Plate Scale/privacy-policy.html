Privacy Policy for Plate Scale

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Plate Scale is a free iOS app from Louis Mercer. It turns a camera's pixel size and a rig's focal length into arcseconds per pixel, sampling, and field of view on this iPhone. Rigs, cameras, and night notes stay on the device. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. Contact Louis Mercer at louis.mercer98@gmail.com. The contact address is United States.
