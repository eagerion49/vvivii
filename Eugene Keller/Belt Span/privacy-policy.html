Privacy Policy for Belt Span

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Belt Span is a free iOS app from Eugene Keller. It keeps each drive’s wheel diameters, center, and the open or crossed belt length on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. A table you choose, and a photo you take of a drive, stay on this iPhone. Contact Eugene Keller at eugene.keller21@126.com. The contact address is United States.
