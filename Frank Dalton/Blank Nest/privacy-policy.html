Privacy Policy for Blank Nest

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Blank Nest is a free iOS app from Frank Dalton. It keeps each sheet’s width, height, blank size, and piece count on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. Contact Frank Dalton at frank.dalton21@sina.com. The contact address is United States.
