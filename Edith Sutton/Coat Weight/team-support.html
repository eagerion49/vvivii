Coat Weight support

Coat Weight is a free iOS app from Edith Sutton. Change the wet film at one station and that station’s dry coat changes. The other stations stay. If the dry coat would leave the window, the stored wet film stays where it was.

Reels stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.
