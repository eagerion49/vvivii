Privacy Policy for Conduit Fill

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Conduit Fill is a free iOS app from Edith Bennett. It checks how full a conduit is on this iPhone. Wire sizes, tubes, and saved fills stay on the device. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. Contact Edith Bennett at edith.bennett28@126.com. The contact address is United States.
