Privacy Policy for Earth Rod

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Earth Rod is a free iOS app from Evelyn Quinn. It reads a ground-rod fall-of-potential test on this iPhone. Rod sizes, probe readings, and saved tests stay on the device. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. Contact Evelyn Quinn at evelyn.quinn43@yahoo.com. The contact address is United States.
