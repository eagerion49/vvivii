Privacy Policy for Rivet Pitch

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Rivet Pitch is a free iOS app from Walter Ramsey. It keeps each rivet row’s diameter, pitch, edge, count, and shear on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. Contact Walter Ramsey at walter.ramsey66@outlook.com. The contact address is United States.
