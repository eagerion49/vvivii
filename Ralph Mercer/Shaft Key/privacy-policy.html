Privacy Policy for Shaft Key

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Shaft Key is a free iOS app from Ralph Mercer. It keeps each key’s width, length, shaft diameter, and the usable moment on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. A table you choose, and a voice note you record, stay on this iPhone. Contact Ralph Mercer at ralph.mercer51@outlook.com. The contact address is United States.
