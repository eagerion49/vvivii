Privacy Policy for Cable Drop

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Cable Drop is a free iOS app from Albert Lawson. It sizes a cable run by voltage drop on this iPhone. Runs, owned sizes, and saved readings stay on the device. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. Contact Albert Lawson at albert.lawson28@163.com. The contact address is United States.
