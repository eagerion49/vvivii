Roll Pass support

Roll Pass is a free iOS app from Albert Nolan. Drag an earlier pass exit and the remaining draft is spread in equal steps down to the target, so the finished thickness stays put. If any pass would not bite, the stored exits stay where they were.

Bars stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.
