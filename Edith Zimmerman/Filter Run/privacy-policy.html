Privacy Policy for Filter Run

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Filter Run is a free iOS app from Edith Zimmerman. It projects when a sand filter should be washed from head-loss readings kept on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The app does not collect data and does not track you. Contact Edith Zimmerman at edith.zimmerman24@outlook.com. The contact address is United States.
